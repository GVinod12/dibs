# Demo imagery

The photographs in this folder are placeholder content for the demo closet,
sourced from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the
[Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license) (free to use, no permission
needed).

They are not the author's own garments — the live deployment uses real photos
uploaded through the owner dashboard, which are stored in Vercel Blob and are
not part of this repository.
